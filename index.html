2 iron vs 5 wood off the tee

2 iron — tight holes, into/cross wind, firm running fairways. Low and penetrating. While the path is being fixed, check your tee-shot data in the app before treating it as the safe option.

5 wood — max carry, soft conditions, long par 3s needing a soft landing, calm days where distance wins.

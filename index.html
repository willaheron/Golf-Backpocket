The system. You're self-directing between occasional lessons. This page is the plan so a quiet few weeks never means starting from scratch. Three pillars, one rhythm, one rule.

Three pillars

1 · Long game — Wednesdays. Climb the lesson ladder on the 7-iron, bed in the driver, sweep the long clubs.

2 · Short game & putting — every second weekend. Open-face wedge work, distance ladders, the putting rebuild.

3 · Play — finish range sessions by actually playing holes, and log real rounds here.

The weekly rhythm

Wed ~1.5hr — long game at Precision (Trackman bay).

Alt. weekend ~1.5hr — short game, chipping green, bunkers, putting.

Optional — a green-only putting touch-up when you can.

The one rule: one swing thought per ball — never two. Pick the block's focus, commit, hit, reset. Stacking cues is how good sessions turn to mud.

When to bring data back to the chat

Driver day — first reshafted-driver session, paste the numbers.

Wedge carries — after a 50/54/58 calibration session.

Every 3–4 Wednesdays — batch your 7-iron exports to check dispersion.

Anything stuck — a number that looks off or a feel that won't come.

2 iron vs 5 wood off the tee

2 iron — tight holes, accuracy priority, into/cross wind, firm running fairways. Low, penetrating, less time in the wind. Your best wind club.

5 wood — max carry, soft conditions, long par 3s needing a soft landing, calm days where distance wins.

What to screenshot / export off the Trackman so numbers are comparable week to week.
